Changes from the draft to the final guidance include: (1) clarifying that the selection of study variables for validation and the extent of effort required for validation depends on the necessary level of certainty and the implication of potential misclassification on study inference; (2) noting that choice of a reference standard for validation may vary by the study design and question, variable of interest, and the necessary level of certainty; (3) recommending the use of quantitative approaches, such as quantitative bias analyses, either a priori for feasibility assessment, or to facilitate interpretation of study results, or for both purposes, to demonstrate whether and how misclassification, if present, might impact study findings; and (4) removing defined terms that are generally understood and transferring other relevant definitions from a glossary to the text. In addition, editorial changes were made to improve clarity.
