    How to judge a call. Adopted: the final moved the way the ask requested and substantially satisfies it. Partially adopted: same direction, narrower or softer. Not adopted: unchanged, opposite direction, or requested content absent. Not assessable: the ask concerns process or things outside the guidance text.

    Concordance, not impact. These calls record whether the final text moved in the direction a commenter asked for. Draft-to-final changes also reflect FDA's internal review, ICH M14, other guidances and evolving science; a matching change does not show that a comment caused it.
