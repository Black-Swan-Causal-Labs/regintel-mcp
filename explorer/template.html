Concordance, not impact

These calls record whether the final guidance text moved in the direction a commenter asked for. Changes between draft and final also reflect FDA's internal review, ICH M14, other guidances and evolving science. A matching change does not show that a comment caused it.

Method

Comments, attachments and both guidance versions were retrieved from regulations.gov by the open-source regintel-mcp server and compared section by section with deterministic code. Claude then classified submitters, split comments into discrete requests and judged each draft-to-final call, following written rules and four human checkpoints. Every request and every evidence passage is quoted verbatim and checked by script.
